## Permission

I agree that **[Show or production company name]** (the "Producer") may:

1. Record my voice, image and likeness during this session (audio and video).
2. Edit the recording for length, clarity and flow, without changing the meaning of what I said.
3. Publish and distribute the recording, and clips from it, in any format and on any platform, worldwide, including for promotion of the show.
4. Use my name, approved bio and approved photo to describe and promote the episode.

## On-tape consent script

Read this at the start of the recording, with the guest answering on tape:

> "Before we start: this conversation is being recorded for [show name] and will be published. Is that okay with you? And can you say and spell your name for us?"

> This is a starting point, not legal advice. Recording, privacy and publicity rules differ between Ontario and the US, so have an Ontario media lawyer adapt it, especially for sensitive topics or minors.
