Growth is where the three disciplines in this guide meet: proven demand ([Validate](../validate/)), a written system ([Operations](../operations/)) and cash discipline ([Money](../money/)). If any one of them says no, wait.

## The gates

| Gate | When you've passed it | Then you can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate 1 | About 40 hours pre-sold within 60 days | Launch lean in your own space |
| Gate 2 | About 20 booked hours a week for 3 months straight, with overhead under 30% of real revenue | Bring in a freelance assistant or engineer on your system |
| Gate 3 | A session runs to standard without you in the room, plus a 3-month cash reserve | Consider a bigger lease or a second room, sized by the lease test |

These thresholds are estimates for a one-room studio. Set your own from the [break-even tool](../../tools/break-even/).

## Stack revenue before you add rooms

Session income alone is fragile. Look for adjacent income your skills and room already support:

- **Mixing and mastering** for remote clients (fills gaps without filling the room).
- **Podcast production retainers** (see [Podcasts](../podcast/)).
- **Video:** session videos and video podcasts.
- **Education:** workshops, home-recording clinics, a course built from your best tutorials.
- **Rentals:** off-peak room rental to other engineers.
- **Sample packs, presets and beats,** with clear licences.
- **Rights income:** producer points and publishing from real co-writes, registered properly.

Test each one with a few paying clients before you build it out.

## Hiring

- **Replace yourself job by job.** Draw the org chart of the finished studio by role (manager, head engineer, assistant, booker, bookkeeper), put your name in every box, then hand them over one at a time.
- **Delegate with a system, never abdicate.** Hand over the checklist with the job, and check the results.
- **Freelancers first.** Use contract engineers and editors with written agreements before you hire staff.
- **Employees** bring obligations: WSIB registration within 10 calendar days of the first hire, minimum wage and payroll deductions. Unpaid internships are generally not allowed in Ontario outside approved school programs.

## Before a second room or a bigger lease

Run every expansion through three questions:

| Question | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Demand | Am I already turning work away? How many hours are pre-sold? |
| System | Can a session run to standard without me? |
| Cash | Does the new overhead pass the 30% lease test on bookings I already have? Is the build-out money already saved? |

## Thinking about selling one day?

A buyer pays for a business that runs without you: clean books, documented systems, a client list that isn't just your friends, and leases or gear in good order. Building that way from the start costs nothing extra.

## Checklist

- [ ] I review my numbers monthly against the gates
- [ ] I've drawn the org chart by role and know which job I'll hand over first
- [ ] Every job I hand over comes with a written checklist
- [ ] I use written agreements with every freelancer
- [ ] I've tested any new service with paying clients before building it out
- [ ] Any expansion passes the lease test on bookings I already have
- [ ] I have a 3-month cash reserve before I take on new fixed costs
